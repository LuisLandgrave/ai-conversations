---

# ⭐ **Example: Insurance Claims Triage (Agentic System vs Human Workflow)**

This is one of the clearest cases where agentic systems _win_ decisively.

## 🧍 **Traditional human workflow**

A human claims analyst must:

- read the claim
- extract key details
- check policy coverage
- verify documents
- classify the claim
- route it to the right department
- flag inconsistencies
- request missing information
- generate a summary
- update the internal system

This takes:

- **15–45 minutes per claim**
- high cognitive load
- repetitive effort
- risk of human error
- inconsistent decisions
- bottlenecks during peak volume

And companies need:

- dozens or hundreds of analysts
- training
- supervision
- QA teams

It’s expensive and slow.

---

# 🤖 **Agentic system workflow (OpenClaw‑style architecture)**

An agentic system can do this in **seconds**, with higher consistency.

### **Step 1 — Document ingestion**

Agent extracts:

- claimant name
- policy number
- incident type
- dates
- location
- required documents

### **Step 2 — Policy lookup**

Agent queries internal systems:

- coverage limits
- exclusions
- deductible
- policy status

### **Step 3 — Classification**

Agent determines:

- claim type
- urgency
- fraud risk score
- required follow‑up

### **Step 4 — Routing**

Agent sends the claim to:

- the correct department
- the correct adjuster
- the correct workflow queue

### **Step 5 — Human‑in‑the‑loop checkpoint**

A human only reviews:

- flagged anomalies
- high‑risk claims
- ambiguous cases

### **Step 6 — Summary generation**

Agent produces:

- a structured summary
- a decision recommendation
- missing‑info requests

### **Step 7 — System update**

Agent writes everything into:

- CRM
- claims system
- audit logs

---

# 📈 **Why this agentic system beats humans**

### **1. Speed**

- Humans: 15–45 minutes
- Agent: 5–20 seconds

### **2. Consistency**

Agents don’t:

- get tired
- get distracted
- forget rules
- misread documents

### **3. Cost**

Agents reduce:

- staffing needs
- training costs
- overtime
- backlog processing

### **4. Accuracy**

Agents catch:

- missing documents
- mismatched dates
- policy inconsistencies
- fraud indicators

Humans often miss these under pressure.

### **5. Scalability**

Agents can handle:

- 10 claims
- 100 claims
- 10,000 claims  
    with no slowdown.

Humans cannot.

### **6. Human‑in‑the‑loop remains**

Humans still:

- approve final decisions
- handle exceptions
- manage disputes
- oversee compliance

Agents do the repetitive work.  
Humans do the judgment work.

---

# 🔥 **Why this works (and why other agentic hype fails)**

Insurance claims triage works because:

- the workflow is structured
- the rules are clear
- the data is standardized
- the agent has guardrails
- the human supervises edge cases

This is the perfect environment for agentic systems.

Contrast this with:

- creative tasks
- ambiguous workflows
- open‑ended reasoning
- unpredictable environments

Agents fail there.

---

# ⭐ **Concise takeaway**

**Agentic systems outperform humans in structured, repetitive, rule‑based workflows — like insurance claims triage — because they are faster, more consistent, cheaper, and scalable, while still keeping humans in the loop for judgment and exceptions.**

This is one of the clearest examples of agentic systems delivering real ROI instead of hype.
